Each year, Medicare reimbursement feels less predictable. In 2026, revenue cycle leaders face a convergence of forces: policy shifts, recalibrated payment formulas, tight telemedicine rules, and payer behaviors that quietly erode margins. Medicare remains the bellwether — and when federal policy changes, commercial payers tend to follow.
